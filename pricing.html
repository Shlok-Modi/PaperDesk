Pricing questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Reach out and we'll help.

Is PaperDesk really free?

Yes — the core paper-trading experience, including live NSE/BSE prices, ₹10,00,000 virtual capital, Watchlist, Portfolio, Orders, and TradeBook, is free with no expiry and no card required.

Is this real money trading?

No. PaperDesk is a simulated environment for educational purposes only — you trade with virtual capital against live market prices, and no real money ever changes hands.

What will Pro include?

Pro is in development and will add unlimited price/indicator alerts, deeper risk analytics, and support for multiple portfolios. Pricing will be announced closer to launch.

Can my college or trading club use PaperDesk?

Yes — reach out about a Campus plan and we'll help you set up group challenges and leaderboards for your members.
