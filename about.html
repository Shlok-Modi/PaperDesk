👋 About PaperDesk

Built so the first real mistake isn't a costly one.

Most people's first encounter with the stock market is also their first time risking real money. We think that's backwards — so we built the practice run first.

Why we built this

Every trader remembers their first order — and for a lot of people, it's also the trade that taught them the most expensive lesson of their life. Market orders that filled at the wrong price. Position sizes that made sense on paper but not in practice. Panic-selling on the first red day. None of that has to be learned with real capital on the line.

PaperDesk exists to move that learning curve somewhere safer — a simulator close enough to a real brokerage terminal that the habits you build here actually transfer, without the downside of getting there the expensive way.

What we believe

  • Practice should feel real. Live NSE/BSE prices, real order types, and a genuine order book — not a toy with fake numbers.
  • Risk-free means risk-free. No hidden funnel into real-money trading, no pressure to "graduate" before you're ready.
  • Understanding beats guessing. Reports and analytics that show you why a strategy worked or didn't, not just whether it did.

Who it's for

Students learning how markets actually move. First-time investors who'd rather practice than gamble. Experienced traders stress-testing a new strategy before committing real capital. Anyone who's ever thought "I should really understand this before I put money into it."
