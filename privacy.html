Privacy Policy

PaperDesk is a paper-trading (simulated) platform. We never ask for your bank details, UPI PIN, or brokerage credentials — there's no real money on the platform, so there's nothing like that to collect.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information PaperDesk ("we", "us") collects when you use our website and app, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using PaperDesk, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and phone number, provided when you sign up or update your profile.
  • Authentication data — a securely hashed password, and, if you use Google Sign-In, the basic profile information Google shares with us (name, email, avatar).
  • Simulated trading activity — your watchlists, orders, positions, holdings, and virtual balance. This data is entirely simulated and has no connection to real brokerage accounts.
  • Usage data — pages visited, features used, and general device/browser information, collected to help us fix bugs and improve the product.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

  • Create and secure your account, and let you log back in.
  • Operate the core product — watchlists, simulated orders, portfolio tracking, alerts, and reports.
  • Send account-related emails, such as OTP verification and password resets.
  • Send price/indicator alert notifications you've configured, including via Telegram if you've linked your account.
  • Understand how PaperDesk is used so we can fix issues and prioritize improvements.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data to trade or make financial decisions on your behalf — there is nothing to trade, since all capital on PaperDesk is virtual.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

PaperDesk uses your browser's local storage to keep you logged in and to remember preferences like your light/dark theme choice. We don't use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

5. Sharing With Third Parties

We share limited data with the following categories of service providers, solely to operate PaperDesk:

  • Market data providers — to power live NSE/BSE quotes and charts shown in the app. We don't share your identity with these providers, only the requests needed to fetch prices.
  • Email & messaging providers — to deliver OTPs, password resets, and (if enabled) Telegram alerts.
  • Infrastructure providers — hosting and database providers that store your data on our behalf, under contractual confidentiality obligations.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

6. Data Security

Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing, never in plain text. We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) between your browser and our servers, and access to production data is restricted to what's needed to operate the service. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information using practices appropriate for the type of data we hold.

7. Your Rights

You can access, correct, or delete your account information at any time from your Profile page, or by contacting us directly. You may also request a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to delete your account and associated data entirely.

8. Data Retention

We retain your account and simulated trading data for as long as your account is active, so your watchlists, positions, and history remain available to you. If you delete your account, we remove your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain limited records for legal or security purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

PaperDesk is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll notify you via the app or by email before they take effect. Continued use of PaperDesk after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out at paperdesk.support@gmail.com.
